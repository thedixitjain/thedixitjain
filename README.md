## 💫 About Me

I'm an **AI Product Engineer** focused on building production-ready Generative AI systems from architecture to deployment.

My work sits at the intersection of **Agentic AI, RAG, MCP, Voice AI, Document Intelligence and Full-Stack AI**, with a strong focus on evaluation, reliability and real business workflows.

I enjoy taking messy real-world problems and turning them into AI products that can handle real users, edge cases and production constraints.

### 🎯 Current Focus

- 🤖 **Agentic AI** - LangGraph, tool calling, stateful workflows, multi-agent orchestration
- 🔌 **MCP & Tool Use** - Model Context Protocol, external tools, APIs and agent integrations
- 🧠 **RAG & Knowledge Systems** - hybrid retrieval, reranking, citations and grounded generation
- 📄 **Document Intelligence** - OCR/VLM pipelines, structured extraction, Text-to-SQL and analytics
- 🎙️ **Voice AI** - conversational agents, speech workflows and real-time business interactions
- 🛡️ **AI Reliability** - evals, observability, guardrails, PII protection, tracing and failure analysis
- ⚡ **Production AI** - async processing, queues, model routing, latency/cost optimization and scalable APIs
- 💻 **AI Product Engineering** - Python backends with TypeScript/Next.js interfaces and production infrastructure
